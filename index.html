Businesses cannot post in our communities without one-time admin approval. That approval is what the program covers, alongside the coaching that makes the posts actually work.

Who it works for

Home services, contractors, auto, cleaning, landscaping, real estate, salons, medical spas, restaurants, event promoters, retail, and anyone selling to local consumers. If your customer lives within driving distance, this fits.

Who it doesn't

If you need a customer in another country, or you sell exclusively business to business at enterprise level, spend your money on search instead. We'll tell you that on the call rather than take it.

How much does it cost?

$75 per month for your first group and $25 per month for each additional group, currently half the standard $150 per group. Twelve-month commitment. Event promotions are $100 per event with no commitment.

Why a twelve-month commitment?

Because short runs don't work and we'd rather not take money for something that won't produce. Local communities buy from names they recognize. Recognition takes months of consistent presence, not a two-week test.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody honest does. What we guarantee is the consultation, the posting access, the featured placements, the admin support, and a capped number of competitors in your category.

Ask on the call. We limit advertisers per industry per group, and some categories are held exclusive. If someone is posting business ads without approval, report them and we remove them.

How often can I post?

As often as you want. The only rules are no stacking posts back to back and no spamming the group. Your consultation covers the cadence that actually performs.

Are the groups public?

No. All groups are private. Posts are only visible to members.
