What you are actually buying

You are buying strategy and access. The strategy is the consultation: a working session with a lead generation expert on how your ads should be structured, when they should run, what makes people respond, and what quietly kills reach. The access is approved sponsored advertiser status, which is what lets a business post at all.

Our communities do not allow business posts by default. Somebody who tries gets a notice explaining that business posts require one-time admin approval. That approval is part of what this program covers, alongside the coaching that makes the posts worth approving in the first place.

This is not a lead list, a directory listing, or a database you get handed. Nothing about it is automated. You post, real local people see it, and the ones who want what you sell contact you directly.

Why local groups work at all

A buy/sell/trade community functions like a general store the whole neighborhood already walks through. People list a couch, a truck, a set of tools, a rental, sometimes a house. Members are not scrolling passively the way they do a news feed — they are shopping, and they are shopping locally. When a plumber, a roofer, a hairdresser or a cleaning service appears in that context and looks like a neighbor rather than a billboard, it does not read as an interruption. It reads as a recommendation.

That is the whole mechanism. There is no algorithm to beat and no auction to win. If your post is good and the group is real, people respond.

The actual craft

What makes a group post work

These are the principles the consultation goes deeper on. They are here because businesses deserve to know what they are buying, and because most posts fail for reasons that are entirely fixable.

01

The first line decides everything

Members see one line before deciding whether to expand. "ABC Plumbing — licensed and insured, serving the area since 2004" gets scrolled past. A specific situation someone is having right now does not. Lead with the problem, not with your credentials.

02

One offer, not a service menu

Listing everything you do reads as a brochure and converts like one. A single concrete offer with a real number attached gives people something to say yes to. Post the other services another week.

03

Photos of your actual work

Stock photography is recognized instantly and it costs you trust. A slightly imperfect photo of a job you finished last Tuesday outperforms a polished image of somebody else's work every time.

04

Write like a member, not a brand

These groups are neighbors talking to neighbors. Marketing voice stands out in the wrong way. Plain first-person language reads as a person who happens to run a business, which is exactly what you are.

05

Cadence beats volume

Posting daily trains people to skip you and irritates the group. A steady rhythm with genuinely different posts builds recognition. Recognition is what makes someone call you six weeks later when they finally need the thing.

06

Answer fast, in the thread

Speed of reply is one of the largest differences between sponsors who do well and sponsors who do not. Public replies also work as proof for everyone else reading, so answer in the comments before moving to messages.

07

Make the next step obvious and small

"Contact us for a consultation" is work. "Comment your zip code and I'll tell you if we cover you" is not. The smaller the ask, the more people take it, and a comment thread pulls in people who were only half looking.

08

Show up as a member too

Sponsors who occasionally answer an unrelated question or help a neighbor find something get treated as part of the community. That standing is worth more than any single post and it cannot be bought.

Ground rules

What we expect from sponsors

These exist so the groups stay worth advertising in. A community full of business posts stops being a community, and then the advertising stops working for everybody in it.

  • No stacked posts. Do not publish back-to-back posts that push other members down the group.
  • No spam. Unlimited posting is a privilege that assumes judgment. Repetitive identical posts are spam regardless of frequency.
  • Approval is required before posting. Business posts without one-time admin approval are removed.
  • Keep it accurate. Real prices, real availability, real photos of real work.
  • Handle replies professionally. Public arguments in the comments cost you far more than the sale you were defending.
  • Report problems rather than fighting them. Message an admin about trolls or unapproved competitors and it gets handled.
